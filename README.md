# Human Resource Dashboard | Tableau Project

Welcome! This repository contains an interactive HR dashboard built with Tableau. The goal of this project was to visualize and explore employee data in a meaningful way to support smarter HR decisions — from hiring trends to salary patterns and performance evaluations.

## 📊 What This Dashboard Shows

The dashboard is broken into key sections that answer real-world HR questions:

### ✅ Overview
A snapshot of the company's current status:
- How many employees are currently active?
- How many have been hired and terminated over time?
- Which departments have the most employees?
- Where are employees located — and how do branches compare to headquarters?

➡️ **Visuals used**: KPIs, line graphs, bar charts, and a map with city/state-level distribution.

**Highlights**:  
Operations and Sales are the largest departments. New York (HQ) accounts for 70% of the workforce, clearly the company’s hub. Hiring rates fluctuate annually, while termination trends are relatively stable.

---

### 🧑‍🤝‍🧑 Demographics
the team:
- What’s the gender ratio?
- How are employees distributed by age and education?
- What’s the connection between education and performance?

➡️ **Visuals used**: Donut charts, bubble matrix, performance heatmaps, and grouped bar charts.

**Highlights**:  
There’s a balanced gender split (54% male, 46% female). Most employees are aged 35–44 and hold a Bachelor's degree. Interestingly, PhD holders show a higher rate of excellent performance.

---

### 💸 Income Analysis
compensation:
- Do salaries differ by education level and gender?
- Are there any visible gender pay gaps?
- What’s the trend between age and salary?

➡️ **Visuals used**: Comparative bar graphs for gender/education, and a scatter plot mapping salary vs. age.

**Highlights**:  
There’s a noticeable gap at the PhD and Master's level, with women earning slightly more. Higher education does lead to higher pay — Finance and IT Managers are among the top earners.
